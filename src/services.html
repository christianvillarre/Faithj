Our Core Specialty / Multifamily Exterior Painting

More Than Exterior Painting — A Complete Exterior Painting Solution.

Multifamily exterior painting is one of Faith J. Construction’s core specialties. We work with apartment communities, owners, property managers, and investment groups on projects of all sizes. What sets our painting service apart is our construction experience: we can handle the exterior repairs a property needs before painting begins, without asking clients to find and coordinate another contractor.

We Prepare the Exterior to Be Painted Right

A lasting paint job depends on the condition beneath the coating. Painting over damaged siding, deteriorated wood, failing fascia, or compromised substrates can leave the underlying problem in place. When our crews find those conditions, we have the construction experience and manpower to address them before the final painting system is applied.

That work can include:

  • Siding, fascia, and soffit
  • Trim, doors, and frames
  • Stucco and woodwork
  • Balconies and railings
  • Caulking and sealants
  • Metal components
  • Damaged substrates, sheathing, and wall components
  • Other exterior repairs needed before painting

Once necessary repairs are complete, our painting crews handle the proper surface preparation, priming, and finish coating. We select coatings for the surfaces involved, protect surrounding areas, and coordinate the work to keep the finished appearance consistent across the property.

One Contractor From Repairs to Final Coat

A traditional painting contractor may be ready to wash, prep, and paint but need to stop when significant repairs are discovered. With Faith J. Construction, the repair and painting work stays under one plan. That means fewer vendors to manage, fewer handoffs, and one team responsible for the completed exterior.

Our construction capabilities support the painting work. We coordinate crews, resident access, equipment, repairs, and coating schedules so the project can move from preparation through final paint with one point of contact and accountability.

Whether a community has a few buildings or hundreds of apartment units, our goal is the same: repair the exterior correctly, prepare it thoroughly, and deliver a professional finish that holds up.
