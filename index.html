About Faith J.

BUILT ON EXPERIENCE. LEAD BY FAITH.

Faith J. Construction is a family-owned and operated general contractor with over 25 years of experience providing construction, remodeling, restoration, and property improvement services.

From new construction and large-scale renovations to interior and exterior improvements, every project is managed with a commitment to quality craftsmanship, clear communication, and dependable execution. We understand that successful projects are built on more than materials—they're built on trust, accountability, and relationships that last.

As a fully licensed and insured contractor, we proudly serve multifamily, commercial, and residential clients throughout Houston and across the United States. Driven by our mission to operate with integrity in everything we do, we approach every job with care, professionalism, and the belief that every task should be completed in good faith. By combining experienced project management, high construction standards, and a relentless focus on customer satisfaction, we deliver spaces that perform better, look better, and stand the test of time.
