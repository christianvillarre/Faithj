About Faith J.

Experience. Integrity. Good Faith.

Faith J. Construction is a family-owned and operated general contractor with over 25 years of experience in construction, remodeling, restoration, and property improvements.

From new construction and large-scale renovations to interior and exterior improvements, we bring quality craftsmanship, dependable execution, and clear communication to every project.

Fully licensed and insured, we serve multifamily, commercial, and residential clients throughout Houston and across the United States. Built on integrity, experience, and lasting relationships, we approach every project with one commitment: to get the job done right and in good faith.
